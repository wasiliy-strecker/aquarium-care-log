# Fotobeispiele

Am 22. Oktober 2026 mit dem eingebauten Imagegen-Werkzeug erstellt.
Synthetische Bilder ohne private Aquarienaufnahmen. In der App ausdrücklich
als KI-generierte Beispiele gekennzeichnet. Nur zur Ansicht.
Keine Übernahme in Pflegeeinträge, Entwürfe oder Backups.

Dateien: `01_aquarium.jpg`, `02_wasserwechsel.jpg`, `03_details.jpg`.
Die ausgewählten Bilder wurden auf 1200 Pixel verkleinert und als JPEG gespeichert.

## Verwendete Prompts

1. Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: bundled example photo for AquariumLog's read-only 'Beispiele ansehen' gallery. Primary request: a clear documentary smartphone photograph showing the whole rectangular freshwater aquarium straight on, aquatic plants, natural gravel, a few small fish and clean glass, modest domestic setting. Landscape 4:3 framing, soft even light, aquarium fully visible, realistic texture. No text, labels, logos, watermarks, UI or collage.

2. Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: bundled example photo for AquariumLog's read-only 'Beispiele ansehen' gallery. Primary request: a clear documentary smartphone photograph of a routine aquarium water change. Close view of a glass freshwater aquarium with plants and gravel, a hand holding a clear siphon tube at the water surface, its hose leading out toward a plain bucket beside the tank. The visible maintenance step is the subject. Landscape 4:3 framing, even natural light, no face, no text, labels, logos, watermarks, UI or collage.

3. Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: bundled example photo for AquariumLog's read-only 'Beispiele ansehen' gallery. Primary request: a close clear documentary smartphone photograph through freshwater aquarium glass showing aquatic plant leaves, natural gravel and the compact dark intake of an aquarium filter. An example of photographing aquarium details clearly for a care journal. Landscape 4:3 framing, gentle even light, realistic texture, no heavy reflections. No text, labels, logos, watermarks, UI or collage.
