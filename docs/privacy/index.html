Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

AquariumLog processes and stores your aquarium and care data on your own device. No account, cloud synchronization, advertising or analytics operated by the app.

This Privacy Policy applies to the AquariumLog mobile app. It explains which data the app handles on your device and when data can leave your device.

“Locally” means processing and storage directly on your own device, where you use the app.

Controller and contact

Responsible: Wasiliy Strecker, AppFabrik AI, Ludwigstrasse 23, 86152 Augsburg, Germany.

Privacy and legal contact: contact@appfabrik-ai.de. User support: support@appfabrik-ai.de.

Data handled by the app on your device

  • Aquarium name, volume, dimensions and location.
  • Care activities, dates and times, time zone information, measurements, units, notes and aquarium details at the time of capture.
  • Captured or selected photos and technical integrity hashes.
  • Reminder settings, form drafts, care reports as PDFs and export information.
  • Encrypted backup files created by the user.

Processing on your device and purposes

AquariumLog processes aquarium details, care entries, measurements, photos, reminders, care reports and backups locally on your device. There is no user account, cloud synchronization, server AI, advertising or analytics operated by the app. App content is not transmitted to AppFabrik AI or to an AppFabrik AI server.

Each list item represents one aquarium. The data is used to document care activities and measurements and create local reminders, PDF reports and encrypted backups.

The Store variant has neither the internet permission nor the permission to access network state. Its own network connections are therefore blocked. The standalone Dev preview uses the same restriction. Dev Debug retains internet permission for a local connection to the Metro development tool. The app does not require an AppFabrik server. Websites opened deliberately are handled by the external browser.

Camera and photos

The camera opens only after a corresponding user action. Gallery content is selected through the system photo picker. The app receives access to the selected file. New care entries may be entered without a photo or with multiple photos.

After capture or selection, the app creates a JPEG photo for private app storage with a longest edge of no more than 1920 pixels and quality level 88. Capture and location metadata are not retained. The source file itself is not modified, but it is not archived as an unchanged camera original inside AquariumLog.

SHA-256 integrity hashes refer to the files stored by the app. They support detection of later file changes and are neither an official timestamp nor a guarantee of evidentiary value.

Device features and permissions

Depending on the features used, the app accesses the camera, photo picker or file picker for backups. Notifications, alarms and system-start events are used only to schedule care reminders selected by the user and restore them after the device restarts. Android permissions determine whether these features are available.

Camera, save-location and sharing actions are initiated by the user. An external link, such as this privacy policy or the GitHub README, opens the corresponding website in the browser.

Export, sharing and recipients

Data leaves private app storage only when the user saves a photo, a care report as a PDF or an encrypted backup to a selected location, sends it to another app or service through the system share sheet, or sends a PDF for printing. The user chooses the destination and recipient. From that point, the privacy terms of the selected storage or communication provider also apply.

AquariumLog does not sell data or share app content for advertising, analytics or profiling.

Security

The app database, photos, attachments and internally stored PDFs are held in private app storage, protected by the operating system. Automatic Android cloud backups and device transfers are disabled for this data. Backups created manually in the app are encrypted with AES-256-GCM and a password selected by the user. Key derivation uses PBKDF2-SHA256 with 600000 iterations. The app does not permanently store that password. Recovery is not possible without the password. Exported PDFs are not encrypted by the backup password.

Backups include saved aquariums, care entries with capture details, reminder settings, photos and PDFs. Unsaved form drafts are not included in backups.

Retention

Your aquarium and care data remains in private app storage on your device until you delete it, clear the app data through device settings, or uninstall the app. AquariumLog operates no server on which this app content is additionally retained.

PDFs and backups stored outside the app remain at the selected location until you delete them there. This also applies to files shared with other apps or people.

Deletion

  • Deleting a care entry removes its data and single-entry reports stored inside the app. Photos are removed when no saved entry or draft still references them.
  • Existing history reports remain separate documents and can be deleted separately.
  • Deleting an aquarium removes its care entries, drafts, reports and reminders.
  • Deleting a care report removes the PDF file stored inside the app.
  • Files stored or shared outside the app must be deleted separately at their respective location.

Because AquariumLog has no account and no server-side storage, no account deletion or server-side deletion request is required.

Contact and support

When you contact us, we process your email address, message and voluntarily supplied information to handle your request. Depending on the request, the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is responding to your enquiry. Recipients may include the email providers used for communication. Support cases are normally deleted twelve months after closure and retained longer only for legal obligations or ongoing claims.

Visiting this website and external links

This privacy policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub processes technical access data, particularly your IP address, to deliver and secure the website. This also happens without a GitHub account. We provide this information page based on our legitimate interest in a secure, publicly accessible privacy policy (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

GitHub receives this data as the hosting provider. Data may also be processed outside the European Economic Area. Information about GitHub's processing, retention, international transfers and safeguards is available in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

We embed no analytics or advertising scripts and no external fonts here. This website receives no aquarium details, care entries, measurements, photos, PDFs or backups from the app. Opening an external link in the app loads the destination website in your browser. Its privacy terms apply.

Your rights

Subject to the GDPR, you have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability and objection, and may lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it for the future. Requests concerning data we process may be sent to contact@appfabrik-ai.de. We cannot access app content stored exclusively on your device. You manage and delete it directly in the app.

Changes
